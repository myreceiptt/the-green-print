# 🍌 Future Proof Crops

**Future Proof Crops** are the crops prepared to grow through future changes.

They are not called future proof because they can predict everything.

They are called future proof because they are designed to stay useful when the season changes.

The future may bring new tools, new platforms, new communities, new problems, new families, new habits, new economies, new blockchains, and new AI systems.

BANANOW cannot control every season.

But BANANOW can prepare its soil.

Future Proof Crops help BANANOW play, learn, and work across culture, media, education, parenting and family support, real-world programs, AI, and Web3.

## Na Now News

**Na Now News** is BANANOW’s media and storytelling crop.

It helps the LAND speak.

It can become a place for stories, updates, notes, reflections, articles, public memory, and digital culture.

Na Now News is not just about being loud.

It is about documenting the LAND while it grows.

A LAND without memory becomes confusing.

A LAND with stories becomes easier to visit, understand, and return to.

## Skool Now

**Skool Now** is BANANOW’s education and program crop.

It is where learning becomes more playful, practical, and alive.

Skool Now can grow into learning programs, community classes, hobby-based education, sports programs, creative skills, and future “XYXYXYXYXYX" Skool Now” concepts.

It connects naturally to the three BANANOW pillars:

- Playing,
- Learning,
- Working.

Skool Now is not only about classrooms.

It is about giving people friendly ways to practice, fail safely, grow slowly, and build skills that can meet the real world.

## Claps

**Claps** is BANANOW’s parenting and family support crop.

It belongs to the AI-aware side of the LAND.

Claps explores how AI can support care, reflection, learning, and communication inside family life without replacing human responsibility.

In BANANOW, family support is not treated as a side topic.

It is part of the soil.

Because the future is not only for builders, traders, founders, developers, or creators.

The future is also for parents, children, caregivers, teachers, and families trying their best every day.

## BANANOW NFT / Heads

**BANANOW NFT / Heads** is BANANOW’s Web3 identity and community participation crop.

BANANOW NFT is connected to Heads, the role for BANANOW NFT holders.

This crop carries the banana on-chain as a collectible, cultural signal, community marker, and piece of digital memory.

Holding a BANANOW NFT should be understood carefully.

It is not a promise of profit.

It is not a promise of revenue.

It is not a promise of marketplace performance.

It is not a guarantee of future benefits.

It is a Web3 participation signal connected to the LAND and the FAMILY.

Any access, activity, utility, or participation connected to BANANOW NFT should only be understood as official when it is clearly announced through official BANANOW channels.

## Future Programs

Future Proof Crops may also grow into other programs when the LAND is ready.

They may involve sports, art, family activities, education, creative economy, Web3 literacy, AI literacy, or new forms that BANANOW has not named yet.

A crop does not need to be forced.

It needs the right season.

The role of BANANOW is to keep the soil ready.
