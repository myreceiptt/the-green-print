# 🍌 Heads

**Heads** are BANANOW NFT holders and Web3 identity participants inside the BANANOW FAMILY.

A Head carries a BANANOW signal on-chain.

That signal may appear as a collectible, a community marker, a cultural symbol, and a piece of digital memory connected to BANANOW LAND.

But this must be clear:

Being a Head is not a promise of profit.

It is not a promise of revenue.

It is not a promise of marketplace liquidity.

It is not a guarantee of future access, rewards, utility, or special benefits.

Being a Head means carrying one part of BANANOW’s Web3 identity layer.

That is meaningful, but it must be understood safely.

## What Heads Carry

Heads carry the banana into the 0101 Universe.

Through BANANOW NFT, Heads help show that culture can live on-chain as memory, identity, and participation.

A BANANOW NFT can be a collectible.

It can be a signal.

It can be a way to say, “I was here in this LAND.”

It can help people learn about wallets, NFT ownership, blockchain records, and Web3 participation.

But it should never be treated as a guaranteed financial instrument.

The banana is playful.

The risk is real.

## Heads and the FAMILY

Heads are part of the FAMILY, not above the FAMILY.

Sprouts matter.

Lovers matter.

Heads matter.

Farmers matter.

Each role helps the LAND in a different way.

A Head may also be a Lover.

A Head may become a Farmer.

A Farmer may also be a Head.

BANANOW does not reduce people into one label.

The role only explains one way someone relates to the LAND.

## Heads and Future Literacy

Heads are connected to the Web3 side of the **Future Literacy Layer (Web3 & AI)**.

Through the Heads layer, BANANOW can introduce people to:

- wallets,
- NFTs,
- digital identity,
- on-chain records,

This is not about hype.

This is about literacy.

The goal is not to make everyone chase the same trend.

The goal is to help people understand what they are holding, what it can represent, and what risks they should respect.

Do not trust random messages.

BANANOW NFT can be playful, cultural, and meaningful.

But every Head should stay alert.

A good Head carries the banana with curiosity.

Heads help BANANOW remember that the digital world can hold culture.
